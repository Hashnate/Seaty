Built for Sri Lankan Operators

Everything your bus company operations need

Engineered specifically for intercity express and luxury bus owners in Sri Lanka. Transition from paper tickets to automated cloud operations.

Fleet Inventory & Seat Maps

Register all luxury coaches, configure custom seat arrangements (2x2, 2x1 luxury recliners), and list vehicle amenities like AC and Wi-Fi.

Automated Recurring Schedules

Define departure rules (Daily, Weekday Express, Weekend Special) once. Seaty automatically generates trips, availability, and passenger booking windows.

Conductor App & Boarding

Equip your conductors with our dedicated mobile app for instant QR ticket verification, live seat boarding status updates, and GPS telemetry streaming.

Live Centralized Fleet Map

Monitor every active bus in your fleet simultaneously on an interactive live map, tracking speed, location, and schedule adherence.

Revenue & Occupancy Analytics

Gain full transparency with real-time financial reporting, seat occupancy ratios, fare collections, and route profitability metrics.

Seamless Vehicle Swaps

Need to substitute a coach for maintenance? Seamlessly assign a replacement bus to scheduled trips without disturbing existing seat holds.
