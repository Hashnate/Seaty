## 1. Who we are

Seaty is operated by **Hashnate** ("Seaty", "we", "us"), #44, 1/1, Main Street, Kinniya,
Trincomalee, Sri Lanka. This policy explains what information the Seaty mobile app and web
services collect, why, and how it's used.

## 2. Information we collect

**Account information.** When you create an account we collect your full name, phone number,
and, depending on how you sign up, an email address and password or a phone number verified by
one-time passcode (OTP). Passenger accounts also collect NIC (National Identity Card) number and
gender where provided, used for seat-gender rules and identity on tickets.

**Booking and payment information.** When you book a trip we store the trip, seats, and price
for that booking, and payment records for completed transactions. We do not store full card
numbers — payment processing details are handled by the payment gateway.

**Location information.** Bus location is streamed from the operator/conductor's device while a
trip is active, so passengers can see the bus's live position and estimated arrival. Passenger
devices do not stream their own location to us — only vehicles being tracked share GPS data.

**Device and notification information.** We store a device push-notification token (Firebase
Cloud Messaging) so we can deliver booking confirmations, trip reminders, and status updates to
your device. SMS confirmations and OTP codes are sent through our SMS provider, Notify.lk.

**Content you provide.** Reviews and ratings you leave for a vehicle, and — for bus operator
accounts — vehicle photos, documents, and company details submitted for verification.

**Usage information.** Standard technical information generated by using the app and API (e.g.
request timestamps), used for operating and securing the service.

## 3. How we use information

- To create and manage your account and authenticate you.
- To process bookings, calculate pricing, and confirm payments.
- To show live bus locations and estimated arrival to passengers on an active trip.
- To send booking confirmations, trip reminders, and status notifications by push notification
  and SMS.
- To let passengers see and leave vehicle reviews.
- To verify bus operators and vehicles before they can accept bookings.
- To operate, secure, and improve the service.

## 4. Who we share information with

- **Bus operators and conductors**, for trips you book with them — your name, gender (where
  relevant to seat rules), and contact details as needed to operate the trip.
- **Notify.lk**, our SMS provider, to deliver OTP codes and booking confirmations.
- **Firebase Cloud Messaging (Google)**, to deliver push notifications to your device.
- We do not sell your personal information.

## 5. Data retention

We retain account, booking, and review information for as long as your account is active and as
needed to meet legal, accounting, or dispute-resolution requirements. You can request deletion of
your account and associated personal data by contacting us (see Section 8).

## 6. Your choices

You can review and update your profile information from within the app. You can request a copy
of, correction to, or deletion of your personal data by contacting us at the details below.

## 7. Children
