Real-Time GPS Tracking

Track your bus live, not the clock

Once your trip departs, the vehicle conductor's device streams live GPS coordinates over a high-speed telemetry feed. You see the coach move on your map in real-time, allowing you to time your arrival at the boarding station perfectly.

  • ✓ Continuous location streaming from origin to destination
  • ✓ Accurate ETA calculations reflecting real highway traffic
  • ✓ Accessible to all booked passengers on the active trip
Digital QR Boarding

Board in seconds without paper tickets

Your validated ticket is generated instantly inside the app upon payment confirmation. When boarding, simply show the dynamic QR code to the Sri Lanka bus conductor for scanning.

  • ✓ Cached offline access inside your mobile wallet
  • ✓ Scanned & validated in less than 2 seconds
  • ✓ Clear seat number, trip ID, and payment breakdown
