Platform Capabilities

Every trip, fully visible & stress-free

Discover how Seaty replaces guesswork with real-time seat availability, live GPS streaming, digital boarding passes, and server-verified security across Sri Lanka.

Live Seat Selection

Pick your exact seat with 100% confidence

Every seat map reflects the true, live state of the bus. Held seats show immediately as held, booked seats lock permanently, and your selection reserves instantly on your screen — powered by real-time WebSocket sync across all users viewing that trip.

  • ✓ Temporary hold timer secures your chosen seat during checkout
  • ✓ Zero double-booking guarantee via server seat lock engine
  • ✓ Layouts match each operator's actual vehicle configuration
Available Held Selected
Real-Time GPS Tracking

Track your bus live, not the clock

Once your trip departs, the vehicle conductor's device streams live GPS coordinates over a high-speed telemetry feed. You see the coach move on your map in real-time, allowing you to time your arrival at the boarding station perfectly.

  • ✓ Continuous location streaming from origin to destination
  • ✓ Accurate ETA calculations reflecting real highway traffic
  • ✓ Accessible to all booked passengers on the active trip
Digital QR Boarding

Board in seconds without paper tickets

Your validated ticket is generated instantly inside the app upon payment confirmation. When boarding, simply show the dynamic QR code to the Sri Lanka bus conductor for scanning.

  • ✓ Cached offline access inside your mobile wallet
  • ✓ Scanned & validated in less than 2 seconds
  • ✓ Clear seat number, trip ID, and payment breakdown
Fare Protection & Security

Transparent server-calculated pricing

Fares, luxury class surcharges, and platform service fees are computed on our secure server API — protecting passengers from unexpected surge fees or client-side tampering.

  • ✓ Immutable server price generation per route
  • ✓ Instant SMS & app booking confirmations
  • ✓ Secure payment processing with official receipts
Verified Passenger Community

Authentic reviews for every luxury coach

Only passengers who completed a trip can publish vehicle reviews. Check ratings for driver courtesy, cleanliness, air conditioning quality, and punctuality before booking.
