Account Management

Delete Your Account

You have the right to delete your Seaty account and all associated personal data at any time. Here's how to do it.

⚠️ This action is permanent.

Deleting your account will permanently remove your profile, booking history, and all personal data from Seaty. This cannot be undone.

How to Request Account Deletion

To delete your Seaty account, please contact us using the method below:

1

Send an Email Request

Email us at support@seaty.lk with your registered phone number and the subject line "Account Deletion Request". We will process your request within 2 business days.

What Data Gets Deleted

Upon account deletion, the following data will be permanently removed within 30 days:

Data Type Action Retention Period
Full name & phone number Permanently Deleted Within 30 days
Booking history & tickets Permanently Deleted Within 30 days
App preferences & settings Permanently Deleted Immediately
Push notification tokens Permanently Deleted Immediately
Financial transaction records Retained (Legal Requirement) 7 years (tax/legal compliance)

Financial transaction records are retained for a minimum of 7 years as required by Sri Lanka's financial regulations. All other personal identifiers are removed from these records.

Need Help?

If you have any questions about account deletion or your data, our support team is here to help.

We respond within 1–2 business days. Please include your registered phone number in your email.
