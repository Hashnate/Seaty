Why Seaty

Everything a smooth journey needs, in one app

Engineered to replace hassle with absolute clarity. From departure schedules to exact seat choices, Seaty elevates intercity highway travel in Sri Lanka.

Live Interactive Seat Selection

Pick window, aisle, or front-row seats on a live layout. Real-time WebSocket synchronization ensures held seats never conflict across users.

Precision Bus GPS Tracking

Track your assigned bus as it maneuvers through highway stops. Receive precise estimated time of arrival (ETA) notifications directly on your phone.

Transparent Server-Side Checkout

Fares and platform fees are generated securely by our backend engine — ensuring absolute price honesty and zero hidden booking surges.

Contactless QR Digital Tickets

Your validated digital ticket lives inside your app. Conductors scan the dynamic QR code in seconds upon boarding for instant admission.

Instant Push & SMS Alerts

Stay informed with automated departure countdowns, delay notifications, platform updates, and digital payment receipts.

Verified Vehicle Reviews

Read authentic feedback from verified passengers on vehicle cleanliness, driver punctuality, and AC comfort before reserving your seat.
